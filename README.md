# Butchery

🔪 What is Butchery?

Butchery is a mod that makes harvesting mobs and animals much much more immersive and fun. Whilst simply killing mobs is still an option with this mod installed, you have the added option of kill a mob with the new Butcher's Knife. Killing a mob with the butchers knife will cause the mobs corpse to drop instead of their original loot (with the exception of sheep, they drop their corpse in addition to their wool). Once you've aquired their corpses you're free to start the butchering process.

🥩 Features

Config file to customise the mod to your specific needs
Kill mobs with the new cleaver to obtain their carcass
Hang corpses from hooks to drain their blood and begin the butchering process
Skin corpses to obtain their skin for converting to leather
Cut up corpses hanging or on the butchers table to obtain different selections of meat (Farmers Delight food can be enabled as an alternative in the config)
Place pig carcass on the rotisserie and a campfire underneath to cook the meat
Fill a mincer with beef or lamb to obtain minced meat, alternatively use the sausage filler attachment and intestines to obtain sausages
Use the hammers to break open turtle shells
Various different decoration blocks including barrel of cod/salmon, jar, brain in a jar, head mounts, cash register and more
Various different armours and costumes
